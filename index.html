    About LeishScan AI •

    An AI-Based Dual-Modality Decision Support System

    LeishScan AI pairs macroscopic lesion imaging with Giemsa-stained microscopy, giving frontline clinicians both visual and parasitological evidence for triaging suspected cutaneous leishmaniasis, even where specialist expertise is scarce.

    The Gap

    A Missing Link in Current Research

    Existing tools rely on single-modality inspection rather than combined clinical and microscopic detection, so lesions that merely resemble leishmaniasis can trigger false positives.

    Our Contribution: a dual-modality AND-gate that requires both lesion and parasite evidence.
